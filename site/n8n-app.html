grvpanchal n8n Google Verified

Secure Google authentication for workflow automation powered by n8n

What is grvpanchal n8n?

grvpanchal n8n is a workflow automation application built by Gaurav Panchal using n8n, an open-source automation platform. It provides secure Google OAuth 2.0 authentication to connect with Google services and automate tasks across multiple Google products.

The app allows authorized users to authenticate with their Google account so that n8n workflows can securely read and interact with Google services on their behalf.

Google Services We Access

When you authenticate with grvpanchal n8n, the app may request access to the following Google services based on the workflows you use:

Google Sheets

Read and write spreadsheet data to automate data collection, reporting, and tracking workflows.

Gmail

Read emails and send automated messages for notification, communication, and email-based workflows.

Google Drive

Access, organize, and manage files in Google Drive for file-based automation workflows.

Blogger

Read and publish blog posts to automate content management and publishing workflows.

How It Works

  1. Sign in with Google — You authenticate securely using Google OAuth 2.0. We never see or store your Google password.
  2. Authorize access — You review and approve the specific Google services the app needs to access.
  3. Workflows run securely — n8n uses your authorized credentials to execute automation workflows on your behalf.
  4. Revoke anytime — You can revoke access at any time from your Google Account Permissions.

Data & Privacy

  • We only request the minimum permissions (scopes) needed for each workflow.
  • Your Google data is processed only within the n8n automation workflows you authorize.
  • We do not sell, share, or use your data for advertising.
  • Our use of Google data adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
  • Read our full Privacy Policy for details.

Who Can Use This?

This app is intended for authorized users invited by Gaurav Panchal to use the n8n automation setup. If you have been given access, you can sign in with your Google account to enable automated workflows.

Security

  • All connections use HTTPS encryption.
  • OAuth 2.0 tokens are stored securely within the n8n instance and are never exposed publicly.
  • The n8n instance is self-hosted and managed by Gaurav Panchal.
  • Access is restricted to invited users only.
